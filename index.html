The College of Computer Studies (CCS) at the University of the Immaculate Conception in Davao City provides premier, industry-aligned computing education designed for 2026 readiness.

Our Academic Programs

BS Computer Science (BSCS)

Focuses on computational theory, algorithmic complexity, and advanced software structures.

BS Information Systems (BSIS)

Bridges the gap between technical business solutions and organizational workflows.

BS Information Technology (BSIT)

Focuses on network administration, security systems, and responsive web applications development.
